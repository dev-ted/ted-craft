# Approach note

One page maximum. Lead with the decisions the reviewer must make. An approval that does not name a decision is a formality, not a review.

Required when the work adds structure, crosses a contract boundary, or is too big to review in one sitting. Below that threshold, the gate stack is the approval.

Approved at triage before the item enters To do. Senior engineers approve. Architecture owners approve when contracts are crossed.
